I build autonomous systems that work in the real world, not just in simulation.

Most recently, I co-led the development of **CTDI's Enzo AMR platform** from scratch, scaling to **125+ robots** across production warehouses in the U.S. and Europe. I owned the full localization and mapping pipeline including LiDAR-based SLAM, scan matching, AMCL, sensor fusion, and drift correction, stabilizing operations across 170,000+ sq ft dynamic warehouse environments.

Before that, I worked at **Bosch on NASA lunar rover programs**, developing smart docking and vision-based navigation systems with high pose estimation accuracy.

> 📰 Featured in [Beckhoff Automation: "Prototype to Production in 60 Days"](https://www.blog.beckhoffus.com/post/prototype-to-production-in-60-days-ctdi-s-enzo-redefines-amr-innovation) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 🎥 [Watch the Enzo AMR in action](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IUHFV2BvJ9w)
